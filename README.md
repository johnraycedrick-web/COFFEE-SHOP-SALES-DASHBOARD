# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales & Branch Performance Dashboard

## Overview
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance, transaction patterns, and branch comparison for a coffee shop business — covering the month of August.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview
![Dashboard Screenshot](https://i.imgur.com/gXJpln2.png)

## 🎯 Business Problem
91% of monthly sales target reached (₱5.25M of ₱5.77M) — ₱520K gap to close within the month.

## 💡 Recommended Resolutions
1. **Bundle & upsell during off-peak hours (11 AM–4 PM)** — pair Croissant/Cheesecake with Americano/Caramel Macchiato
2. **Day-specific promos for Tuesday & Sunday** — via GCash/Cash, paired with a "Double Stamp" Loyalty Card (2x stamps on Tue/Sun)
3. **Reallocate discount budget by ROI** — shift from strong days to low-performing days/hours

**Target impact:** ~₱480K–550K in added sales — enough to close the ₱520K gap.

## Key Metrics (August)
- **Sum of Net Sales:** ₱5.25M
- **Avg Daily Sales:** ₱174.92K
- **Next Month Target:** ₱5.77M
- **% of Goal Reached:** 90.91%
- **Sum of Discount:** ₱91.97K
- **Required Daily Average:** ₱192.42K

## Key Features
- Sales tracking by product, category, day, hour, and branch
- Payment method breakdown (Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, GCash, Maya)
- Branch comparison and weekly filtering
- Interactive slicers for branch, week, day, and category filtering
  
## Key Insights
- Coffee dominates sales at 72.9% (₱3.83M) — Pastry, Non-Coffee, Frappe, and Dessert combined make up only 23.7%
- Friday posted the highest net sales (₱947.85K); Tuesday, Wednesday, and Sunday showed the lowest — opportunity for targeted promos
- Transaction peaks occur around 8–9 AM and 6–7 PM, indicating morning and evening rush
- Americano and Caramel Macchiato are the top-performing products
- GCash + Maya are the leading payment methods (50.4% of net sales combined)
- Branch A (₱2.61M) and Branch B (₱2.64M) are nearly even in performance
- A ₱520K gap remains to reach next month's target
